About the strategy

✓

The strategy's rules are private. It decides once a day and trades Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana against USDT on Binance spot.

✓

No borrowing: at most 100% of the account is invested. No margin, no leverage.

✓

Trade-only access: the bot's Binance key can place trades but cannot withdraw funds.

✓

Rules fixed in advance: fingerprinted before testing and not changed since.

The account trades a small amount of real money on Binance spot. Values are the bot's own budget: its Bitcoin plus its cash, priced at the Binance mid price at each daily run. Deposits, withdrawals and budget changes are recorded as money put in or taken out, not as gains.

Simulated results show how the rule behaved on past prices and do not guarantee future returns. The strategy can lose money; in simulation its largest fall from a high was about 30%. This page is a record of results, not investment advice.
